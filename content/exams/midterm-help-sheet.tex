\textbf{Minor}\\
The minor of the element $a_{ij}$, denoted by $|M_{ij}|$, is obtained by deleting the $i$th row and $j$th column of the matrix and taking the determinant of the resulting matrix.

\textbf{Cofactor}\\
The cofactor $|C_{ij}|$ is defined as:
\[ |C_{ij}| = (-1)^{i+j} |M_{ij}| \]

\textbf{Determinant}\\
For an $n \times n$ matrix, expanding with respect to the \textit{first row}:
\[|A| = \sum_{j=1}^n a_{1j} |C_{1j}| \]

\textbf{Adjoint}\\
The transpose of the cofactor matrix $C = [|C_{ij}|]$:
\[adj A = C^T = \left[\begin{array}{llll}
|C_{11}| & |C_{21}| & \cdots & |C_{n1}| \\
|C_{12}| & |C_{22}| & \cdots &  |C_{n2}| \\
\vdots &\vdots & \cdots &  \vdots \\
|C_{1n}| & |C_{2n}| & \cdots & |C_{nn}| \\
\end{array}\right]\]

\textbf{Inverse}\\
Divide the adjoint of $A$ by the determinant of $A$:
\[ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{|A|} adj A\]

\textbf{Cramer's Rule}\\
Given $Ax = b$, form the matrix $A_k$ by interchanging the $k$th column of $A$ with $b$; then
\[x^*_k = \frac{|A_k|}{|A|}\]
