\item[3.] Given the function $y=5 x-2$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the difference quotient $\Delta y / \Delta x$. What type of function is it?
\item[(b)] Since $\Delta x$ does not appear in $\Delta y / \Delta x$ in part (a), does it make any difference to the value of $\Delta y / \Delta x$ whether $\Delta x$ is large or small? Consequently, what is the limit of the difference quotient as $\Delta x$ approaches zero?
\end{enumerate}
