A point $x_0$ is a critical (or stationary) point of $f$ if $f'(x_0) = 0$.

Find all the critical points of the following function:
\[ f(x) = x^3 - 12x^2 + 36x + 8 \]

\vspace{6cm}

Denote the two critical points by $x_1^*$ and $x_2^*$, with $x_1^* < x_2^*$.

\begin{itemize}
  \item What is the sign of $f'(x)$ for $x < x_1^*$ in the immediate neighborhood of $x_1^*$?
  \vspace{1.2cm}
  \item What is the sign of $f'(x)$ for $x > x_1^*$ in the immediate neighborhood of $x_1^*$?
  \vspace{1.2cm}
  \item What is the sign of $f'(x)$ for $x < x_2^*$ in the immediate neighborhood of $x_2^*$?
  \vspace{1.2cm}
  \item What is the sign of $f'(x)$ for $x > x_2^*$ in the immediate neighborhood of $x_2^*$?
\end{itemize}

\newpage
Using the first-derivative test, which critical point gives a relative maximum and which gives a relative minimum? What are the values of $f$ at these points?

\vspace{4.5cm}

The second derivative of a function $f(x)$, written $f''(x)$, is the derivative of $f'(x)$. Find $f''(x)$ and evaluate it at $x_1^*$ and $x_2^*$. What does the second-derivative test tell you about each critical point? Does it agree with your answer above?

\vspace{5cm}

Sketch the graph of $f(x) = x^3 - 12x^2 + 36x + 8$ for $0 \leq x \leq 8$, marking the two critical points.
