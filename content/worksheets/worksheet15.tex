The second derivative of a function $f(x)$, written $f''(x)$, is the derivative of $f'(x)$.

On Monday you found the critical points $x_1^* = 2$ and $x_2^* = 6$ of
\[ f(x) = x^3 - 12x^2 + 36x + 8 \]
Find $f''(x)$. What are the values of $f''(x_1^*)$ and $f''(x_2^*)$? What does the second-derivative test tell you about each critical point?

\vspace{7cm}

Is $f$ concave, convex, or neither (a) on the whole real line, (b) for $x \geq 4$?

Given the quadratic profit function
\[ \pi(Q) = hQ^2 + jQ + k \]
what parameter restrictions are called for to reflect the following assumptions?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] If nothing is produced, the profit will be negative (because of fixed costs).
  \vspace{2.5cm}
  \item[(b)] The profit function is strictly concave ($\pi''(Q) < 0$ for all $Q$).
  \vspace{5cm}
  \item[(c)] The maximum profit occurs at a positive output level $Q^*$.
\end{itemize}
