Consider the function
\[ f(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2 - 3x \]

\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] Find $f_x$ and $f_y$, and use the first-order conditions to find the critical point.
  \vspace{6cm}
  \item[(b)] Find $f_{xx}$, $f_{yy}$, and $f_{xy}$.
  \vspace{3.5cm}
  \item[(c)] Is the critical point a maximum, a minimum, or neither? What is the value of $f$ there?
\end{itemize}

\newpage

A firm sells two products in quantities $Q_1$ and $Q_2$, and its profit is
\[ \pi(Q_1, Q_2) = 12Q_1 + 18Q_2 - 2Q_1^2 - Q_1 Q_2 - 2Q_2^2 \]

\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] Write the first-order conditions and solve for the output levels $Q_1^*$ and $Q_2^*$.
  \vspace{7cm}
  \item[(b)] Use the second-order conditions to check that this is a maximum. What is the maximum profit?
\end{itemize}
