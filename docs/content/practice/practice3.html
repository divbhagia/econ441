Exercise 4.2

1. Given \[A=\begin{bmatrix}7 & -1 \\ 6 & 9\end{bmatrix}, \quad B=\begin{bmatrix}0 & 4 \\ 3 & -2\end{bmatrix}, \quad C=\begin{bmatrix}8 & 3 \\ 6 & 1\end{bmatrix},\] find:

  1. \(A+B\)
Solution

\(A+B=\begin{bmatrix}7 & 3 \\ 9 & 7\end{bmatrix}\)

  1. \(C-A\)
Solution

\(C-A=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 0 & -8\end{bmatrix}\)

  1. \(3A\)
Solution

\(3A=\begin{bmatrix}21 & -3 \\ 18 & 27\end{bmatrix}\)

  1. \(4B+2C\)
Solution

\(4B+2C=\begin{bmatrix}0 & 16 \\ 12 & -8\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}16 & 6 \\ 12 & 2\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}16 & 22 \\ 24 & -6\end{bmatrix}\)

2. Given \[A = \begin{bmatrix}2 & 8 \\ 3 & 0 \\ 5 & 1\end{bmatrix}, \quad B=\begin{bmatrix}2 & 0 \\ 3 & 8\end{bmatrix}, \quad C=\begin{bmatrix}7 & 2 \\ 6 & 3\end{bmatrix}:\]

  1. Is \(AB\) defined? Calculate \(AB\). Can you calculate \(BA\)? Why?
Solution

\(AB\) is defined, since the number of columns in \(A\) is two, which equals the number of rows in \(B\). \[AB=\begin{bmatrix} 2 \times 2+8 \times 3 & 2 \times 0+8 \times 8 \\ 3 \times 2+0 \times 3 & 3 \times 0+0 \times 8 \\ 5 \times 2+1 \times 3 & 5 \times 0+1 \times 8 \end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix} 28 & 64 \\ 6 & 0 \\ 13 & 8 \end{bmatrix}\]

Not possible to calculate \(BA\): \(B\) has two columns, but \(A\) has three rows.

  1. Is \(BC\) defined? Calculate \(BC\). Is \(CB\) defined? If so, calculate \(CB\). Is it true that \(BC=CB\)?
Solution

\(BC\) and \(CB\) are both defined, as both are \(2 \times 2\). \[BC=\begin{bmatrix} 14 & 4 \\ 69 & 30 \end{bmatrix} \neq CB=\begin{bmatrix} 20 & 16 \\ 21 & 24 \end{bmatrix}\]

4. Find the product matrices in the following (in each case, append beneath every matrix a dimension indicator):

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix}0 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 & 0\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}8 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 3 & 5\end{bmatrix}\)
Solution

\(\begin{bmatrix}0 & 2 \\ 36 & 20 \\ 16 & 3\end{bmatrix}_{3 \times 2}\)

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix}6 & 5 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & 4\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}4 & -1 \\ 5 & 2 \\ 0 & 1\end{bmatrix}\)
Solution

\(\begin{bmatrix}49 & 3 \\ 4 & 3\end{bmatrix}_{2 \times 2}\)

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix}3 & 5 & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & -7\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x \\ y \\ z\end{bmatrix}\)
Solution

\(\begin{bmatrix}3x+5y \\ 4x+2y-7z\end{bmatrix}_{2 \times 1}\)

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix}a & b & c\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}7 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \\ 1 & 4\end{bmatrix}\)
Solution

\(\begin{bmatrix}7a+c & 2b+4c\end{bmatrix}_{1 \times 2}\)
